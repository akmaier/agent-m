# SPEC approvals — queue 2026-10-15 · the prompt bar

**PO decision:** a person talks to a participant in one prompt bar at the bottom of every page, which gives the
participant the page as context. Before anything is sent, the page names where it goes — the participant and the place
where it processes data —, not what is sent, since a participant that uses tools decides while it works what it reads.
A conversation is held in the browser tab: it is a job that leaves no job record, and none of its messages is written to
a repository unless the instance switches on recording conversations in git, which is off unless switched on. Work a
conversation hands to CI is a job with a record that names its inputs without the conversation's text.

**Size:** three entries. 01, §7 *Configuration and secrets*: `THE PAGE STATES WHAT IT SENDS WHERE` is withdrawn and `THE
PAGE NAMES WHERE IT SENDS` is added in its place. 02, §9 *Human gates*: `A RECORD IS EVIDENCE, NOT A PROPOSAL` names
conversation records. 03, §13 *Process execution and jobs*: `A JOB IS RECORDED IN ITS PRODUCT REPOSITORY` exempts a
conversation held in a browser tab, and three new requirements follow it — `A CONVERSATION IS WRITTEN TO A REPOSITORY ONLY
WHEN RECORDING IS ON`, `A RECORDED CONVERSATION IS KEPT WHERE IT WAS HELD` and `SWITCHING CONVERSATION RECORDING ON STATES
WHAT FOLLOWS`. Every other requirement of the three sections is carried over byte for byte. Drafted with it: the new
UC-049, and UC-003, UC-017, UC-019, UC-020 and UC-042; twelve further use cases change only the name in their `realises`.
Accept the queue first, then the use cases; the architecture follows against the accepted use cases.
