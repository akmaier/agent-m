# Source put in front of the quoted rule name

PO instruction, 2026-09-23: *"In the cases where the source is missing, the source is PO."*

The four requirements here kept a quoted rule name, for example "JEDE SPEC-AENDERUNG LAEUFT UEBER
DAS FREIGABE-WERKZEUG", but lost its author and date when the path to the internal process
repository was removed. The quote names the rule they were taken over from. It does not say who
decided or when, so it is not a source in the sense of §3. The proposal keeps the quote and puts
the PO and the date in front of it, for example: `*(PO A. Maier, 2026-09-23; after "JEDE …")*`.
If you want the quote gone entirely, delete it in the field; the text is then taken as you leave it.
