    MOD-documents.

  (MOD-spec-document, Data).
  - No test asks `parseSpec` to tell a process requirement from a product one, and the outcome drops "whether each
    constrains the product or the development process". Instead, a test reads this instance's SPEC.md with its
    `sources`; MOD-product-process takes its requirements as the process requirements.
  - A requirement missing a field is read with that field `null`. Naming it as a finding is the job of
    `requirementFindings`, which this item does not build.
- ITM-215:
  - Each rule the module's file names for `modelFindings` gets a definition that breaks it, including the four the item's

- ITM-217: MOD-source-register checks no participant's place. `permittedPlaces` gives the places; checking a participant
  against them is the job of `eligible` (ITM-216, `Need.places`) and `declarationFindings` (ITM-218).
  - The criterion "a participant's place checked against them" leaves the item.
  - Its tests state `permittedPlaces` for four sources: one that may be republished (`any`), a restricted one with
    places, a restricted one with none, and one whose licence is `unknown`.
