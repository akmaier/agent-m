**What went well.**
- The sprint delivered its goal: UC-002 runs on the dashboard, with release tests by a participant who implemented none of
  it.
- The developers built only what their items named and noted each gap in their pull requests; no item stopped for a
  drafted change, and the one change request got one review.

  they used about 305,000, 539,000 and 419,000 tokens, the opus developers of this sprint about 297,000 to 567,000 per
  item. Whether the model and the reading save cost is to be measured over the next sprint, not claimed from three items.
- The gates caught two real omissions (#136, #138) before they reached `sprint/06`.

  and an import of a module's private file.
