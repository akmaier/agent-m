- An implementation job changes only code in the folders of its modules and tests that name them (`AN IMPLEMENTATION JOB
  CHANGES ONLY ITS MODULES`, which `docs/process.md` makes the Definition of Done). The re-exports in old files under
  `docs/assets/` that ITM-203 to ITM-207 asked for lie outside it; the items now ask for the module and its tests. No page
  of the dashboard calls a module of this sprint until a change between jobs makes it, and that change waits for
  akmaier's decision on the queue `2026-10-06_what-no-module-owns`, as does the entry of MOD-repository-hosts in the
  `fetch` list of `tests/test_no_backend.py`, without which ITM-205's CI stays red. ITM-206 builds on ITM-205, and
  ITM-208 on both.
- ITM-204: MOD-browser-store keeps every setting under the prefix `agent-m:<owner>/<repository>:`, one JSON value per key
  of its catalogue; the dashboard's pages keep theirs under `agent-m.` (`docs/assets/settings-store.mjs`) — the GitHub
  token and its expiry under two keys, every GitLab token in one map —, and the tests of those keys, release tests of
  sprints 01 and 02 among them, hold that layout. A page on the module's store would not see what the other pages store,
  nor they what it stores. The pages move to the module's store together, with the settings pages, which this sprint does
  not do.
- ITM-207: the add-product page is one of those pages and stands on the module's store; it goes with them.
- ITM-208 builds on ITM-205 and ITM-206 instead of ITM-207, and checks UC-001 through the dashboard's add-product page as
  it calls their modules.
- Of the goal, this selection reaches, once the pages call the modules: UC-001 reads and writes the product, and writes
  its review layout, through the modules of ITM-205 and ITM-206, while the products and tokens it keeps in this browser
  stay in the dashboard's store until the settings pages move; and the architecture page draws the component diagram of
  ITM-203.
