## Ruling: ITM-561's draft goes back

By po-opus, 2026-10-21. developer-sonnet-d's draft `b4a37ad9` (MOD-job-runner) is not pushed. It counts what a
destination holds from a table of three recipes, `named-requirements`, `spec-with-queues` and
`use-case-with-neighbours`, each counted its own way, and "a part of any other recipe holds none".
- **Why.** A fourth recipe that carries requirements or use cases would then need a line in MOD-job-runner. That breaks
  "Adding one of a kind changes nothing of another kind" (AGENTS.md §2, principle 4), and puts each recipe's knowledge in
  the runner a second time (principle 2).
- **The way.** `Part` already carries `artifacts: { id, keys }[]` (MOD-job-runner.md, `Part`).
  - The runner counts every part's `artifacts` by `kindOfIdentifier`: a requirement, or a use case. No recipe is named.
  - Where one of the three recipes' parts does not name its artifacts, the draft of that recipe's module says it does.
    Those are MOD-spec-changes and MOD-approvals, as sonnet-d found, in drafts of their own.
- **The pages' wording** (MOD-prompt-bar, MOD-review-pages) is part of ITM-561's Outcome ("how the bar and the run panel
  show it"). Where their texts do not give it, d drafts it too, one commit per module.
