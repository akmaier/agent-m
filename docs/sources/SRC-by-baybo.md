# SRC-by-baybo BayBO — Bayerische Bauordnung

The Bavarian building code, chosen as the state whose law the test project follows. Art. 55 is the basic permit duty, Art. 57 lists the works that need no procedure (a fence, TC-B1), Art. 10 covers structural stability (load-bearing walls are out of scope).
