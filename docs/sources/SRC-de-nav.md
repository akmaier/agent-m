# SRC-de-nav NAV — Niederspannungsanschlussverordnung

Who may work on an electrical installation behind the grid connection: § 13 limits the work to the grid operator and to companies in a grid operator's installer register. Backs the 230/400 V scope exclusion and SAF-02.
