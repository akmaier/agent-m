# SRC-by-agbgb AGBGB — Bayerisches Ausführungsgesetz zum BGB

The Bavarian act that carries out the Civil Code. Its articles 43 to 54 are the Bavarian neighbour law (window rights, boundary distance of plants, limitation of neighbour claims): the 'check locally' rules of AIR-10 for a garden project.
