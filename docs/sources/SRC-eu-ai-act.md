# SRC-eu-ai-act EU AI Act — Regulation (EU) 2024/1689

The AI Act. D.AI.Y is an AI system that talks with people, so the transparency duties of Art. 50 are the first to check; Art. 6 with Annex III is read to rule out a high-risk classification. The amendments of the AI omnibus are not in this version and are added as a new version once the amending text is registered.
