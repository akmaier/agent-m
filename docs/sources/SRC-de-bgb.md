# SRC-de-bgb BGB — Bürgerliches Gesetzbuch

The German Civil Code. The rental-law sections say what a tenant may change in a flat (G1, TC-A4, AIR-10 'check locally'); §§ 903, 921, 922 and 1004 are the parts of its neighbour law that a boundary fence (TC-B1) touches.
