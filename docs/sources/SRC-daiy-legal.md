# SRC-daiy-legal D.AI.Y legal and standard texts

The laws, regulations and the accessibility standard that bound D.AI.Y, as the extracts of the parts the product links (GDPR, AI Act, F-gas regulation and certification, BGB, NAV, NDAV, SchfHwG, 1. BImSchV, TDDDG, BayBO, AGBGB, WCAG 2.2) and, as advisory background, the two Wikipedia articles on the five safety rules and on the Elektrofachkraft, in one source so that one derive run reads them together. Each extract's heading names its law: a candidate's passage says which law it is from.
