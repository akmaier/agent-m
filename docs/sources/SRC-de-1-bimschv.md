# SRC-de-1-bimschv 1. BImSchV — Verordnung über kleine und mittlere Feuerungsanlagen

The first ordinance to the Federal Immission Control Act: scope, definitions (among them a 'substantial change' of a firing system), the inspection of new and substantially changed systems, the recurring inspection and the notification duties. Backs the same scope exclusion as SchfHwG.
