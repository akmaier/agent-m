# SRC-wikipedia-fuenf-sicherheitsregeln Wikipedia — Fünf Sicherheitsregeln

A free explanation of the five safety rules of electrical work (isolate, secure against reconnection, verify absence of voltage, earth and short-circuit, cover neighbouring live parts), with the legal bases and standards by country. It stands in for the DGUV brochure, whose terms forbid this use, and backs the wording of the hazard warnings (SAF-02). It is a secondary source.
