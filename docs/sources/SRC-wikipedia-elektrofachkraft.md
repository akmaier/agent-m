# SRC-wikipedia-elektrofachkraft Wikipedia — Elektrofachkraft

A free explanation of who counts as a qualified electrician (Elektrofachkraft) in Germany and which norms and rules define it. It backs the line between work a layperson may do and work that needs a qualified person (FR-14, SAF-06, SAF-07). It is a secondary source; the binding rule is NAV § 13.
