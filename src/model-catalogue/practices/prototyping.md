# Prototyping

**REGISTER**

A practice of the book *Vibe Coding* as this instance's source register holds it (`SRC-vibe-coding`, version
`2026-10-05`): chapter 14, *Change Management with Prototyping and Incremental Delivery*, and its Figure 14.4. It is
added to a declared model and replaces none. It fits every model of the catalogue: it belongs to the chapter's strategy of
evolution, whose framing "applies equally to plan-driven and agile development environments".

## Adds

Four steps before the team commits to full-scale implementation, each with its output:

1. *Objectives* — what the prototype should demonstrate, and which decisions it informs: a prototype plan.
2. *Functionality* — which features the prototype includes, and which it leaves out: an outline.
3. *Develop* — a slice of real code, or a simulation, depending on the uncertainty: an executable prototype.
4. *Evaluate* — the users' feedback, and whether the original questions are answered: an evaluation report.

## What it is

A prototype is not a way to build the final product quickly. It is an instrument for discovering what stakeholders
actually need before the team commits to building it: users try integrated behaviour instead of reading a specification,
and often find that they want something different from what they first described — which is what a prototype is for.
Learning through a prototype costs far less than building a complete system that does not meet the actual needs. Book
chapter 14.
