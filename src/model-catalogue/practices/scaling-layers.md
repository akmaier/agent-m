# Scaling layers

**REGISTER**

A practice of the book *Vibe Coding* as this instance's source register holds it (`SRC-vibe-coding`, version
`2026-10-05`): chapter 7, *Scaling Agility to Large Systems*, and its Figure 7.5, after Ambler. It is added to a declared
model and replaces none. It fits the agile models, Scrum and Kanban: "One common answer nests agile practice in
increasingly disciplined layers."

## Adds

Two layers around core agile development — short cycles, close feedback, working increments:

1. *Disciplined agile delivery* — stronger coordination and delivery control.
2. *Agility at scale* — a broader governance framework for multiple teams and organisational layers, often with
   plan-driven elements higher up in the hierarchy: Scrum-on-Scrum structures, additional planning layers.

## What it is

Agile methods were designed for small teams in close proximity. Once a system becomes large, distributed, contractual or
safety-relevant, informal interaction no longer tells the groups who is doing what, which artifact is authoritative and
which commitments are contractually meaningful — and the communication paths grow faster than the team. The scaling
layers surround core agile practice with additional structure. Three groups of factors decide how much: on the project
side, system size, system type, expected lifetime and regulations; on the development side, team skill, tooling maturity
and the capabilities of the AI agents; on the management side, contracts, delivery expectations and work culture. Book
chapter 7.
