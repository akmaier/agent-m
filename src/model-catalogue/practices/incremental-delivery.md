# Incremental delivery

**REGISTER**

A practice of the book *Vibe Coding* as this instance's source register holds it (`SRC-vibe-coding`, version
`2026-10-05`): chapter 14, *Change Management with Prototyping and Incremental Delivery*. It is added to a declared model
and replaces none. It fits every model of the catalogue: where contracts, regulation or coupled legacy dependencies
constrain the work, "incremental delivery is possible but requires more upfront planning, formal approval gates, and
integration verification than greenfield agile development".

## Adds

- Usable portions of the real system shipped to the stakeholders, in place of throwaway demonstrations: each iteration a
  step toward the final product.
- The users' feedback on the system's integrated behaviour, from which they refine their requirements.
- Under contracts, regulation or coupled legacy dependencies: more upfront planning, formal approval gates and
  integration verification, and a longer rhythm of delivery.

## What it is

Incremental delivery complements prototyping: instead of building a prototype, throwing it away and starting the real
implementation from scratch, each iteration ships a usable part of the real system. Stakeholders receive value earlier and
give their feedback on what the system actually does, not on a specification; this improves the requirements, reduces
misunderstandings, and often lowers the total cost. Book chapter 14.
