Welcome to Agent M

Agent M runs the software engineering cycle of the book Vibe Coding on your own repositories — requirements, use cases, architecture, implementation, tests and release — with agents doing the work and people deciding at every gate.

Start with the requirements and the use cases: each is reviewed and accepted on its own page. Then add the repository of a product you build.

What is this?

This page shows where each product of this instance stands on its way through the process: one bar of six stages, each filled by the share of its work that is accepted or done. While this browser manages no product, it shows Agent M itself, on its way to its first release.

Everything is read from the repositories when the page opens; nothing is stored. Your products and your token live only in this browser. The menu above leads through the process in its order; each entry opens the page where that stage's work is reviewed and done.
